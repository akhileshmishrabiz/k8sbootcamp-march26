## LivingDevOps

A real-world DevOps education platform built to "train the new army of DevOps, SRE and Platform engineers to tackle the challenges of tech in the AI age."

- **Pedagogy:** Live production scenarios, real deployments, live troubleshooting (debugging things in real time as they break), over slides-and-definitions tutorials.
- **Curriculum themes:** Linux, Docker, Kubernetes/EKS, Terraform, Python, CI/CD, GitHub Actions, ArgoCD, observability (Prometheus/Grafana), incident response, MLOps, AIOps.
- **Student outcomes (from testimonials):** transitions from cloud-support roles into DevOps, interview success, job-readiness for production environments.

## Content & teaching themes

Recurring topics across his writing and posts:

- **Kubernetes as narrative** — "Every Kubernetes concept has a story"; the K8s ecosystem exists because "Kubernetes alone wasn't enough."
- **Senior vs. junior thinking** — root-cause investigation and mental frameworks over reactive log-chasing; methodology as the real differentiator.
- **Learning philosophy** — motivation and deadlines beat procrastination; "you can learn anything if you're motivated enough."
- **Practitioner-first, no-fluff voice** — skeptical of surface-level learning; "learn it like a DevOps engineer," not "for dummies."
- **Pragmatic tooling takes** — prefers Mac, convenience over ceremony; the core tools (Linux, Docker, K8s, Git, Terraform, Prometheus) are free — the bottleneck is doing the work.
